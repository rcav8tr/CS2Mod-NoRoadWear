# No Road Wear
Completely eliminate road wear.

# Description
Are you tired of slow-moving road maintenance vehicles causing traffic jams?
This mod is for you!

Normally, roads accumulate wear due to time and traffic.
With this mod, all existing road wear is removed when a city is loaded (new or saved)
and all further accumulation of road wear is prevented.
Working road maintenance vehicles will eventually return to the depot on their own.
After loading your city, display the Roads infoview to see that all your roads are in top condition.
Roads in top condition allow for higher speeds and have fewer accidents.

Road Maintenance Depot vehicles have only three purposes:  repair roads, plow snow, and clear traffic accidents.
- Because roads have no wear, road maintenance vehicles will not be dispatched to repair roads.
- Snow does not cause road wear.
  However, snow plows repair road wear the same as road maintenance vehicles.
  Therefore, snow plows will not be dispatched to repair roads or plow snow.
- You may need only one or a few Road Maintenance Depots to provide road maintenance vehicles to clear traffic accidents.

If you use this mod together with a mod that prevents traffic accidents
(like the **Disable Accidents** mod by Azsaidi with the Disable All Accidents option turned on),
then you can delete all your Road Maintenance Depots because the depot vehicles will have no purpose and will never be displatched.

This mod does NOT change how road wear is displayed on roads.
This mod only changes the game's internal tracking of wear on roads.
There are other mods that change how road wear is displayed on roads.

# Options
This mod has no options.
If this mod is subscribed and enabled, then it is working.

# Compatibility
This mod is compatible with the **Road Builder** mod.
Roads constructed with **Road Builder** will have no wear.

This mod is compatible with the **Parks, Roads, Lane Wear** mod.
If both mods are enabled, this mod takes precedence to completely eliminate road wear.
You can still use this mod with the **Parks, Roads, Lane Wear** mod for its other features.

This mod is compatible with the **Snowy Roads** mod with a caveat.
Snowy Roads uses the presence of a snow plow on a road to clear snow from the road.
Because this mod prevents snow plows from being dispatched,
the **Snowy Roads** mod will never clear snow from roads (i.e. roads will remain snow covered).
If this acceptable, you can still use this mod with **Snowy Roads** for its other features.
Snow on roads is only a visual effect and does not otherwise affect the road.
Snow on roads will eventually clear on its own when the weather warms.

There are no known compatibility issues with any other mods.

This mod can be safely disabled or unsubscribed at any time.
Roads will accumulate wear again due to time and traffic.
